
============================================
  LIVE ROOM CONVERSION ANALYSIS — SUMMARY
============================================
1. CATEGORY: Talent & Dance rooms earn 2-3x more per session
   than Game or Music rooms. Chat is in the middle.

2. PK EFFECT: Rooms with PK enabled earn ~85% more revenue
   and have 70% higher interaction density vs non-PK rooms.
   However, only 20% of rooms use PK.

3. PEAK HOURS: Revenue peaks at 14-22 UTC, aligning with:
   - Middle East evening (UTC+3: ~17-01 local)
   - SEA night (UTC+7: ~21-05 local)
   Suggests scheduling premium content in this window.

4. REVENUE CONCENTRATION (Power Law):
   - Top 5% rooms capture ~55% of total revenue
   - Median room: $1,662/day; P95: $13,000+/day
   - Severe skew: mean is 2x median

5. CROSS-REGION: Rooms with 20-30% cross-region viewers
   earn 40% more than region-locked rooms.

RECOMMENDATIONS:
- Encourage PK usage with incentives; current 20% rate is low
  relative to massive revenue uplift
- Schedule top anchors in 14-22 UTC window
- Invest in "cross-region" discovery algorithms
- Focus operational support on top 5% rooms (Pareto principle)
